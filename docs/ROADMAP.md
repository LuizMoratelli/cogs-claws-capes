# Roadmap: from punching trees to your first hero

Every suit, power, gadget and the Omnitrix needs a **Hero Core**, and a Hero Core needs Create, fossils and the Nether.
Tick the boxes as you go. Press **R** on any item in JEI to see its exact recipe, and **U** to see what it is used in.

## 1. Survive and settle
- [ ] Wood, stone tools, a bed, food (Farmer's Delight gives good food early)
- [ ] Iron tools and armor
- [ ] A base near water and ores, claimed on the map (`M` -> right-click chunks)

## 2. Create, first steps (rotation)
- [ ] **Andesite Alloy** (andesite + iron nuggets, or zinc nuggets)
- [ ] A **Water Wheel** or **Windmill** for power, plus **shafts** and **cogwheels**
- [ ] **Mechanical Press** (plates and sheets), **Encased Fan** (washing / smelting in bulk)
- [ ] Find **zinc** ore (it generates in its own veins)
- [ ] **Mechanical Mixer** + **Basin**

## 3. Brass (Create mid-game)
- [ ] Catch a blaze into an **Empty Blaze Burner** (Nether fortress) -> **Blaze Burner**
- [ ] **Brass Ingots**: copper + zinc in a **heated** mixer (basin on a Blaze Burner)
- [ ] **Brass Casing**, **Electron Tubes**, **Deployers**, **Mechanical Crafters**

## 4. Precision Mechanism
- [ ] **Golden Sheets** (press gold ingots)
- [ ] A **sequenced assembly** line (a press or deployers on a belt) that makes **Precision Mechanisms**
      (golden sheet + cogwheel + large cogwheel + iron nugget, several loops; some come out as scrap)

## 5. Dinosaurs: the fossil part of the Hero Core
- [ ] Mine **fossil** blocks underground (Fossils & Archeology): they drop **Bio Fossils**, bones and relics
- [ ] Keep at least **3 Bio Fossils per Hero Core**
- [ ] Optional now, fun later: Analyzer -> DNA -> Culture Vat -> your own dinosaurs

## 6. Nether supplies
- [ ] **Blaze Rods**: 3 per Hero Core (and more for Blaze Burners)
- [ ] **Diamonds**: 3 per Hero Core

## 7. The Hero Core line (the big goal)
A sequenced assembly on a **Precision Mechanism**, **3 loops** of:
**deploy Bio Fossil -> deploy Diamond -> deploy Blaze Rod -> press**
- [ ] Build it with 3 Deployers + 1 Mechanical Press on a belt (JEI: R on "Hero Core")
- [ ] First Hero Core
- [ ] Automate it: every suit and power costs one

## 8. Become a hero
- [ ] **Tailoring Bench**: made in **Mechanical Crafters**, needs a Hero Core, brass, planks, shears and **fabrics**
      (Palladium fabric: wool + sticks + string, any color)
- [ ] **Your first suit** at the Tailoring Bench (each suit costs another Hero Core)
- [ ] **Powers and gadgets** (serums, DNA, Compound V, Iron Man add-ons...): Mechanical Crafters or a **heated Mixer**,
      each with a Hero Core
- [ ] Keys: powers on the ability bar (`V B N M ,` by default, or `Z X C V B` with the hero keys); `X`/`G` next page

### Iron Man (Satsu) also needs research
- [ ] Craft a **Recipe Table** (8 paper + blue dye), hold it, open **Powers** in the inventory
- [ ] Buy the tiers with **XP levels** (a Mark like IX needs Medium Armor/Flight/Weapons/Portability, about 137 levels)
- [ ] Marks are tailored with Iron Man parts (alloys, circuits, arc reactor) + a **Stark Hammer** + a Hero Core
- [ ] The bracelet goes in the **Curios suit slot**, not the chest slot

## 9. Ben 10
- [ ] Explore **Galvan / machine structures** (crash sites, Galvan mines, pods, machine towers)
- [ ] **Brush** suspicious sand and gravel there: **Galvan Scrap** and alien **DNA codes**
- [ ] **Omnitrix Workbench**: iron blocks + Galvan Scrap + an upgrade crystal + a crafting table (Hero Core gated)
- [ ] **Prototype Omnitrix**: Galvan metal + upgrade chips + a prototype core (Mechanical Crafters + a Hero Core)
- [ ] Unlock aliens with the codes you find

## 10. Endgame (when you feel strong)
- [ ] Iron's Spells: Inscription Table / Scroll Forge / Arcane Anvil (each needs a Hero Core)
- [ ] Bosses: L_Ender's Cataclysm, Mowzie's Mobs, Ice and Fire dragons
- [ ] Trains (Steam 'n' Rails), cannons (Big Cannons), jetpacks

*Recipes come from the pack's own data (KubeJS gating in `overrides/kubejs/server_scripts/hero_gating.js`). If something
here does not match JEI, JEI is right - tell the server owner.*
